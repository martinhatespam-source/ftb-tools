For the Bucks tools

For the Bucks tools is a private desktop tool. It uploads and manages videos for one YouTube channel, For the Bucks, a comedy channel about money.

The channel owner is its only user. The tool is not offered to anyone else, and it does not access any other channel or account.

What it does through the YouTube Data API:

  • uploads the channel's own videos
  • sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, subtitles and publish times
  • posts the channel's own comments
  • manages its playlists
  • reads the channel's own video list and view counts
